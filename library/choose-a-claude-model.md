Claude comes in three sizes. Pick by the job, not by habit.

- *Haiku*: the fastest and cheapest. Use it for quick, simple tasks such as short rewrites and lookups.
- *Sonnet*: the everyday choice. Use it for most work, including drafting, analysis and code.
- *Opus*: the most capable and the slowest. Use it for hard, multi-step problems where a wrong answer costs time.

If an answer is weak, try the next size up before rewriting your prompt. Using all three is not a checklist item; the glossary explains the terms.
