Welcome. I am the Onboarding Copilot. I help new practice members start using Slack, Claude and Context Builder.

Here is what I will do with you:
- I will ask your role once, in my next message, so that I give you the right steps.
- I give you one step at a time, the simplest first.
- I tick a step myself when I can see you have done it. When I cannot, I ask you to confirm it.
- You can ask me "how do I..." at any time. I answer only from the practice's onboarding library, and I say so when it has no answer.

The glossary explains the Claude terms you will meet, such as model, effort, Project and skill. Keep it open while you work.
